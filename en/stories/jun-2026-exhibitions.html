2026 / 6, Photo Exhibitions of the MonthSix Closing This Month, Five Running Into Summer, and Come Back Home

The second weekend of June is the busiest. Six shows closing within the month, five running into summer, and the Seoul Photo Festival 〈Come Back Home〉, back after five years. Dates and routes, all in one place.

5ft.mag Editors 2026.06.06

This article was translated from Korean with AI. Read the original in Korean →

"June always brings a pile of month-long shows. On the same weekend, one closes and another opens. The second weekend is the busiest."

That's true every month, but more so this time. The Seoul Photo Festival, back after five years, has two weeks left, and alongside it a line of short- and mid-run group shows are opening and closing in step. One route can decide your whole month. We start with what ends within the month.

Six Closing Within the Month

Kim Nam-ho, Jo Seong-gyeong, Park Tae-jin, 〈Seeing Han Kang: Yeosu, Evening, Vegetarian〉
Busan Gallery. 6.2 – 6.14. Artist talk Sat 6.6, 14:00.
A group show that rereads Han Kang's writing through photography. Kim Nam-ho turns the short story 〈Love of Yeosu〉 (여수의 사랑) into 50 black-and-white photographs, Park Tae-jin renders Yeong-hye from 〈The Vegetarian〉 in intense color, and Jo Seong-gyeong carries the poetry collection 〈I Put the Evening in the Drawer〉 (서랍에 저녁을 넣어두었다) into images of red sunsets and evening. Curated by Kim Nam-ho.

Youngkwi Choi, 〈MONOLOGUE〉
Gallery Toma (Daegu). 6.2 – 6.17. Opening 6.2, 5:00 PM.
The tone of someone talking to himself. The main image is the back of a person standing in the rain. A solo show.

Jo Young-shin, invitational exhibition, 〈Small Faces, One World〉
Riart Center, 1F (16-3 Gung-dong, Dong-gu, Gwangju). 6.4 – 6.26.
Faces at a small scale. Gathered, they become one landscape. Scattered, each becomes its own world. Curated by director Namgung Yo.

2026 KOWPA exhibition 〈Surfaces Desiring Depth〉
Artists: Kang Ji-mi, Kim Mi-ja, Lee Young-hwa, Jeong Ok-young, Ha Ji-young, Blonde Jenny. Curators: Lim An-na, Han Ki-ae. Organized by the Korean Women Photographers Association (KOWPA).
Ha Gallery (8-45 Mabang-ro 6-gil, Seocho-gu, Seoul). 6.4 – 6.13. 12:00 – 18:00. Opening 6.4, 5:00 PM.

〈Objects in Oscillation〉
Artists: Koo Bohnchang, Koo Sung-yeon, Kim Kyoung-tae, Kim Su-kang, Park Chan-woo, Jeong Jeong-ho, Jung Hee-seung, Cho Sun-hee, Jo Sung-yeon.
Kukje Gallery. 6.9 – 7.19.
Objects seen in a state of vibration rather than in the form of still life. Nine contemporary photographers pick up the texture of objects that Koo Bohnchang has worked with for years. It doesn't close within the month, but it sits right in the middle of June's flow, so we grouped it here.

Kim Mi-jeong, Lee Seon-hwa, 〈OLD PARIS〉
Lookinside Gallery (2F, 30-1 Dosan-daero 17-gil, Sinsa-dong, Gangnam-gu, Seoul). 6.17 – 6.29. Opening 6.20, 5:00 PM.
Two photographers taking a second look at Paris. In what tone did they carry over the face of the old city?

Public Programs and Festivals

Jo Mun-ho, 〈1987: How Mr. J of Yeokchon-dong, a Seoul Citizen, Joined the Democracy Movement / How the World Transforms〉
Gallery Index (3F, 45 Insadong-gil). 6.3 – 6.15. Reception 6.3, 5:00 PM.
Jo Mun-ho's record of following one man through June 1987. That June, seen again from a single citizen's point of view.

Chunsoo Kim, 〈The Future Won't Happen on Its Own〉
Amado Art Space. 5.29 – 6.28.
The gap between the passing of time and the future. Time passing doesn't mean you arrive.

We put the Seoul Photo Festival 〈Come Back Home〉 at the top of this piece (Photography Seoul Museum of Art, 4.9 – 6.14). It is back after five years. It treats home as more than a place to live: a place where memory, relationships and feelings pile up. Twenty-three artists are grouped into four sections: "What Makes a Home," "The Moving Home," "On the Road" and "Our Home." The second weekend of June (6.14) is the last chance.

Five Running Into Summer

〈elegy of beauty〉
Artists: Linda Connor, Mary Daniel Hobson, Sal Taylor Kydd, Christina McFaul.
datz museum of art. 4.4 – 8.2.
Four photographers each bring their own answer to the question "What is beauty?"

Max Siedentopf, 〈SERIOUSLY NOT SERIOUS〉
Groundseesaw Central. 3.27 – 8.30.
The Dutch-born artist's first large solo show in Asia. Moving across photography, video, sculpture and installation, he uses the cover of humor to take another look at what reality is made of.

Thierry Ardouin, 〈SEED STORIES〉
GoEun Museum of Photography (Busan). 4.23 – 8.30. Marking the 140th anniversary of Korea-France diplomatic relations.
Images of seeds from around the world, magnified with a scanning electron microscope, cut across agriculture, institutions and human desire.

Jang Nam-won, 〈Moving Island, Whale〉
Solgeo Art Museum, Gyeongju. 5.11 – 9.13.
Korea's only whale photographer, who has followed whales for some thirty years since 1992. The show gathers a lifetime of work. In the 1980s, when Korean underwater photography centered on close-up work, he was the one who came in with a wide-angle lens.

"The second weekend is the busiest this month. 〈Surfaces Desiring Depth〉 closes first on June 13, followed the next day, the 14th, by 〈Seeing Han Kang〉 and 〈Come Back Home〉, and by 〈1987〉 on the 15th. On the third weekend 〈OLD PARIS〉 opens and 〈MONOLOGUE〉 wraps up."

At a Glance

  • This weekend (6.6 – 6.7) Saturday afternoon: artist talk for 〈Seeing Han Kang〉 (Busan Gallery, 6.6, 14:00).
  • Second weekend (6.13 – 6.14) The busiest weekend. 〈Surfaces Desiring Depth〉 (Ha Gallery, 6.13), 〈Seeing Han Kang〉 (Busan Gallery, 6.14), 〈Come Back Home〉 (Seoul Photo Festival, 6.14) and 〈1987〉 (Gallery Index, 6.15) close one after another.
  • Third weekend (6.20 – 6.21) 〈OLD PARIS〉 opens 6.20. 〈MONOLOGUE〉 closes 6.17.
  • Fourth weekend (6.27 – 6.28) 〈Small Faces〉 (6.26), 〈OLD PARIS〉 (6.29) and 〈The Future Won't Happen on Its Own〉 (6.28) all wrap up the same week.
  • Into summer 〈elegy of beauty〉 (datz museum of art, 8.2), 〈SERIOUSLY NOT SERIOUS〉 (Groundseesaw Central, 8.30), 〈SEED STORIES〉 (GoEun Museum of Photography, 8.30), 〈Moving Island, Whale〉 (Solgeo Art Museum, Gyeongju, 9.13) and 〈Objects in Oscillation〉 (Kukje Gallery, 7.19) give you another chance in July and August.

This list draws on the June curation by the Instagram accounts @sajinyesul_photoart and @soonjeong.class. Check each gallery's hours and closing days before you go. If we confirm any changes, we will update this piece.
