May 23, a Saturday on Eoulmadang-ro

The sixth Film Camera Flea Market, hosted by Saeki P&C. Cameras, film and coffee gather at five spots in a single alley.

Film Social Club 2026.05.19

This article was translated from Korean with AI. Read the original in Korean →

Past seasons fit on a few tables inside one shop. This time, five spots across three shops in one alley open at once. You can walk from stop to stop, drifting between cameras, film and coffee. Along the way you'll also find 5ft magazine issues 1 and 2 and three books from the SPC photobook series.

At a Glance

  • Name: Film Camera Flea Market, Season 6: "Find your Film Vibe"
  • Date: Saturday, May 23, 2026
  • Time: 10:30~16:00
  • Host: Saeki P&C
  • Location: Around Eoulmadang-ro and Yanghwa-ro, Mapo-gu (between Hapjeong and Sangsu)

Three Stops, Five Spots

Saeki P&C + Seogyo-dong 398 Cafe

36 Eoulmadang-ro 5-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul. Two spots in one building, set up so you can go from looking at cameras straight to a cup of coffee.

  • Saeki P&C: a longtime hub for photo and video equipment distribution in Korea, and the host of this market. A shop where you can get your hands on cameras, film and printing supplies all in one place. @saekipnc · saeki.co.kr
  • Seogyo-dong 398 Cafe: a neighborhood café in the same building. Think of it as the pause in your market route. @cafe398

Darkroom Seoul (DARK ROOM IN SEOUL)

2F, 26 Eoulmadang-ro 5-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul. A workroom with everything from film developing, scanning and printing to self-service studio shoots. A floor where "making photographs" really fits. darkroomseoul.co.kr

Day for Record (DAY FOR RECORD)

2F, 57-10 Yanghwa-ro 6-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul. Between Hapjeong and Sangsu, a workroom-slash-café known for developing motion picture film. They rent cameras too, so you can start a roll even if you show up empty-handed. @dayforrecord

Sellers

Besides the four shops above, one individual seller is joining in.

  • Kim Tae-pung and the Kids (김태풍과아이들): a table spread with film cameras and odds and ends. @k_typhoon12

See and Buy On Site

Along the flea market route you'll find 5ft magazine issues 1 and 2 and three books from the Street Photography Club photobook series. They'll be on display and for sale. It's a rare chance to flip through each printed issue in one place.

Getting There

Saeki P&C, Seogyo-dong 398 and Darkroom Seoul are all in the same alley on Eoulmadang-ro 5-gil. Only Day for Record is a block away, toward Yanghwa-ro 6-gil. Everything is within walking distance of both Hapjeong and Sangsu stations. Doors open at 10:30 AM and close at 4 PM, so plan to make a loop with lunch somewhere in the alley.

Whether you're bringing home a new camera or letting go of one that's been sitting on a shelf, it makes for a short Saturday-afternoon stroll.
