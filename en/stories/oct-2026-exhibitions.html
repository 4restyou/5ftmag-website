2026 / 10, Photo Exhibitions of the MonthA Wetland, a Coal Mine, a City and a Photo Festival in One Month

October's list reaches further outside Seoul than inside it. It starts at Upo Wetland and moves on to a new solo show by Yoo Seung-ho, the city of Busan, a photo festival in Jinju and a record of coal mines. We list the shows in order of closing date.

5ft.mag Editors 2026.10.01

This article was translated from Korean with AI. Read the original in Korean →

"This month, the places come first. A wetland, a mine, a city, a street of cinemas, a regional photo festival. Before you read what the photographs show, you read where they stayed for a long time."

October brings short solo shows that close quickly alongside regional exhibitions that run into November. In the first week, check Gaon Gallery in Insa-dong and Space Isin first. Goodspace Gallery and Gallery AP_9 close mid-month. At the end of the month come Yoo Seung-ho's 〈FACES OF ALL THINGS〉 and Canon Gallery, and Photo Jinju carries on into early November. The long-running shows by Min Byung-hun and Lee In-mi don't need to be rushed, but they make good anchors when you plan your October route.

Closing First

Jeong Bong-chae, 〈向: Drawn on the Water〉 (向, 물위에 그려지다)
Space Isin. 10.1 – 10.13.
For Jeong Bong-chae, Upo Wetland has long been a place for thinking, more than a landscape. This show concentrates on the marks that form on the water. The path the wind took, the edges left by melting ice, the debris and nets floating on the surface: at some point they turn into lines and signs. The 向 in the title means direction, but the directions in these photographs never point to a destination. The show makes you ask less where you should go than where you are standing now.

Lee Ju-hyeong, 〈Bloom and Boundary〉
Goodspace Gallery. 9.15 – 10.14.
A solo show that works through azaleas to catch moments of light and sensation. The flowers are the subject, but the point is less the flower itself than the edge of the moment it opens. It closes in mid-October, so on a Seoul route it is one to see early.

Lee Jae-yong, Lee Gwan-yong and Kim Mi-rim, 〈Reading Film Through Photography: From Gosa-dong to Hollywood〉 (사진으로 읽는 영화: 고사동에서 헐리우드까지)
Gallery AP_9. 9.18 – 10.16.
Here photography and film read each other. The Gosa-dong in the title ties back to Jeonju's movie street. The photographs don't trace scenes from films. Think of it instead as a show that rereads, in photography's own terms, the places and memories that films leave behind.

Through the End of October

Yoo Seung-ho, 〈FACES OF ALL THINGS〉
Comeamico Gallery (꼬메아미꼬 갤러리). 10.21 – 10.27. 97 Seongmisan-ro 15-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul.
A solo show gathering photographs taken from 2019 to 2026. It follows 〈BLUE HOUR〉, the film photography show Yoo held in early October, so you can see the two back to back in the same month. Our story on 〈BLUE HOUR〉: read it here →

〈STILL CHUBBY: Just Like This, for a Long Time〉 (STILL CHUBBY - 이대로 오래)
Canon Gallery. 9.13 – 10.31.
Just as the title says, a show about staying "just like this, for a long time." It runs at Canon Gallery through the end of October. It is held in a camera brand's space, but it reads less like a showcase of finished results and more like a look at how photographs linger in everyday life.

Into November: Major Shows and Regional Exhibitions

2026 Daegu Photo Biennale special exhibition, 〈Red Flags Become Stars〉 (붉은 깃발 별이 되어)
Daegu Culture and Arts Center. 9.11 – 11.1.
We covered this show in last month's article. Read it in 2026 / 9, Photo Exhibitions of the Month →

Photo Jinju 2026 (사진진주 2026), 〈Relationships: Connecting with the World〉 (관계, 세상과 연결되다)
PAC Gallery and three other venues. 10.18 – 11.1.
A photo art festival in Jinju, started in 2017. This year's keyword is "relationship." The program widens its view from a person's inner life to the ways that person connects with the world. We always like it when a regional photo festival opens several spaces across one city. It is better seen on foot around town than in a single day of viewing.

Park Byeong-mun and Yasmeyando, 〈Lives at the Coalface〉 (막장 광부들의 삶)
Samuño Valley Mining Ecomuseum. 9.30 – 11.1.
Park Byeong-mun is a documentary photographer who has spent many years recording Korea's coal-mining towns and the lives of their miners. This show sets mines in Korea and Spain side by side, along with the lives of the people who worked in them. Even the venue's name carries the memory of the place. It makes plain why photographs need to survive as a record.

〈Photo World〉 (사진 세계)
Hyundai Children's Book Art Museum. 7.21 – 11.1.
A show that explores the many worlds layered inside photographs. Since it is at a children's book museum, the way in is easy, but it also shows first-time viewers how photographs build worlds and how those worlds are read. If you are going out with kids, it is worth fitting in before the end of October.

Shows You Can Take Slowly

Min Byung-hun, 〈BETWEEN FORMS〉
Particle, House of Photography. 9.18 – 12.20.
Min Byung-hun's photographs always hold back from saying too much. Hazy light, low contrast, forms that remain only just, as if about to vanish. As the title suggests, this is a place to look at what lies between one form and the next. It runs through December, so there is no hurry, but on a day you want quiet black-and-white photographs, it is the surest choice in October.

Lee In-mi, 〈FLUID SCAPE〉 (흐르는 도시 FLUID SCAPE)
GoEun Museum of Photography. 9.16 – 12.26.
The GoEun Museum of Photography's Busan Project 2026. Here the city is a flowing landscape, not a fixed backdrop. It is a long-running photo show in Busan, so if you have a Busan trip planned in October, you can put it ahead of Canon Gallery or the other regional shows.

Route Notes

In Seoul, the first two weeks of October are what count. Lee Gyeong-taek's 〈Zero Point〉 (Zero Point -영점-) closes first, at Gaon Gallery on October 5. Jeong Bong-chae closes on the 13th, Lee Ju-hyeong and the two-person show at White Wave Gallery on the 14th, and Gallery AP_9 on the 16th. To see them together, start with Insa-dong, Space Isin and Goodspace Gallery.

We already covered the Daegu Photo Biennale special exhibition last month, so for October just check the link. The new anchor is Jinju, from October 18. If you go for Photo Jinju, plan it more like a walk around the city than a day of exhibition-going.

Seoul is worth one more visit in late October. Yoo Seung-ho's 〈FACES OF ALL THINGS〉 runs at Comeamico Gallery from the 21st to the 27th, and 〈STILL CHUBBY〉 at Canon Gallery is up until the 31st.

In Busan, Lee In-mi's show at the GoEun Museum of Photography runs long, through December. If you have a Busan trip in October, give it time and take it slowly.

October's shows don't rest on one big blockbuster. They are built from places someone kept looking at for a long time. The surface of Upo Wetland, the coalface of a mine, the flow of a city, the slow buildup of a region's photo history. In the end, a photograph can't hide where it lingered.
