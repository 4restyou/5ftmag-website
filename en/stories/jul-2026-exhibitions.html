2026 / 7, Photo Exhibitions of the MonthThree Closing in July, Three Running Through Summer, and a New Museum in Busan

In July, the last weekend is the busiest. Three shows that close within the month, three that carry on through the summer, and Korea's first Ralph Gibson museum, quietly opened in Busan. Dates and routes, all in one place.

5ft.mag Editors 2026.07.09

This article was translated from Korean with AI. Read the original in Korean →

"July is unusually full of solo shows. Faces gathered over twenty years, the terrain of the moon, a documentary photography award arriving in Gwangju. And beside them stand the big exhibitions that run through the whole summer."

If June was crowded with month-long group shows, July puts solo shows and award exhibitions up front, shows that take a long look at one person's time. Some run three weeks, others until next spring, and they all overlap in the same month. One last weekend decides July. We start with the shows that end within the month.

Three Closing in July

Kwak Myung-woo, 〈Photo Sea: Twenty Years of Faces〉 (사진바다: 스무 해의 얼굴들)
SPACE 22 (22F Mijin Plaza Building, 390 Gangnam-daero, Seoul). 7.2 – 7.22. Opening 7.4 (Sat) 4:00 PM. Mon–Sat 11:00 – 19:00 / closed Sundays and public holidays.
Faces he has set in front of the camera over twenty years. This solo show gathers more than two decades of seeking out people in one place. The people sitting side by side on the bench in the poster stand in for the faces of that time.

Choi Donghun, 〈Selenography〉
Gallery Toma (Daegu). 7.11 – 7.26. Opening reception 7.11 (Sat).
The title comes from selenography, the study that maps the surface of the moon. The artist, who is also a reconstructive plastic surgeon, reads "surface" and "map" on top of each other. Lunar craters and skin, aerial photographs and the grain of tissue all become terrain in a single frame. Curated by Un.Charted Foundation.

2026 Onbit Photography Award, Category Winners' Exhibition
Gallery Hyeyum (Gwangju). 7.14 – 7.26. Organized by ONBIT.
The show brings together this year's four category winners of the Onbit Photography Award, which has long stood by documentary photography: Kim Heung-gu, 〈Teumeong: Island, Stone, Face〉 (트멍: 섬, 돌, 얼굴) (Fujifilm Photography Award); Kim Da-min, 〈I AM YOU〉 (Sealy Photography Award); Lee Jong-su, 〈Landscapes of Absence〉 (부재의 풍경들) (Haewon Emerging Photographer Award); and Lee Myeong-geon, 〈By Bike〉 (Haewon University Student Photography Award). It is a touring show that began at Artspace Lumos in Daegu (6.10 – 6.27), moves through Hyeyum in Gwangju, and continues to Igong Gallery in Daejeon (9.8 – 9.21). This summer's stop is Gwangju.

Three Running Through Summer

Ralph Gibson, 〈French Allure〉
GoEun Gibson Museum of Photography (10 Jungdong 1-ro 37beon-gil, Haeundae-gu, Busan). 6.17 – 2027.4.10. Tue–Sun 10:00 – 18:00 / closed Mondays. Admission 3,000 won.
The opening exhibition of Korea's first permanent Ralph Gibson museum, in Haeundae, Busan. Timed to the 140th anniversary of Korea-France diplomatic relations, it gathers the work he made in France. The question is how a master of American photography turned French surfaces (velvet, gilt, shadow) into his own language. It is open until next spring, so it is worth adding to a summer trip to Busan.

Martin Parr, 〈We Are Martin Parr〉
SeMA Museum of Photography (Seoul Museum of Art). 7.16 – 10.18. Free admission.
The Magnum photographer died last December. This is the first large-scale retrospective in Asia since his death. Some 500 works from his early black-and-white to his late color, 14 major series and the 89 photobooks he made over his lifetime are brought together in one place. Work he shot in North and South Korea in the early 2000s is also shown for the first time. Co-curated by Magnum Photos and the Martin Parr Foundation.

Kim Jeon-gi, 〈Figures of Memory〉 (기억의 형상들)
Gangneung Museum of Art. 7.22 – 8.4.
He follows the artificial sculptures put up at tourist sites: a Marilyn Monroe statue, a golden horse, a soldier. Figures made to imitate someone's memory sit out of place in real landscapes, and the result is a slightly lonely, unfamiliar "landscape of memory." It is only open through the first week of August, so it is best to catch it in July.

"In July, the last weekend is the busiest. Kwak Myung-woo's twenty years close on the 22nd, and on the 26th Choi Donghun's moon and the Gwangju stop of the Onbit Photography Award close on the same day. In between, on the 22nd, Kim Jeon-gi opens in Gangneung."

Summary

  • This weekend (7.11 – 7.12) Opening reception for 〈Selenography〉 (Gallery Toma, Daegu, 7.11 Sat). On the same day, the Onbit Photography Award show in Gwangju is still a few days from opening.
  • Third weekend (7.18 – 7.19) The best time for the Onbit Photography Award (Gallery Hyeyum, Gwangju). Kwak Myung-woo's 〈Photo Sea〉 (SPACE 22) closes very soon, on 7.22.
  • Last weekend (7.25 – 7.26) The busiest weekend. Choi Donghun's 〈Selenography〉 and the Onbit Photography Award winners' show both close on 7.26. Kim Jeon-gi's 〈Figures of Memory〉 in Gangneung opens on 7.22, so this weekend is the first chance to see it.
  • Through the summer Ralph Gibson's 〈French Allure〉 (GoEun Gibson Museum of Photography, to 2027.4.10), Martin Parr's 〈We Are Martin Parr〉 (SeMA Museum of Photography, to 10.18) and Kim Jeon-gi's 〈Figures of Memory〉 (Gangneung Museum of Art, to 8.4) are still open after the start of August.

This list draws on the July curation by Instagram account @sajinyesul_photoart and the official channels of each museum and gallery. Dates follow the official channels. Please check opening hours and closing days before you go. If we confirm any changes, we will update this article.
