Fujifilm Neopan 400 Presto

Fujifilm's everyday black-and-white film. Its fine, clean grain helped shape the feel of Japanese black-and-white photography. Discontinued in 2013, it's mentioned alongside Acros II more often than any other Japanese black-and-white film.
