Ilford Ilfocolor 400 Plus

A color negative carrying the Ilford name, best known for black-and-white. It's now sold as Ilfocolor Vintage Tone 400 Plus. The color leans slightly toward cyan, so greens and blues are subdued while reds and yellows stand out, and true to the name, it's described as giving the look of old photographs. C-41 process, daylight balanced, 35mm in 24 and 36 exposures.
