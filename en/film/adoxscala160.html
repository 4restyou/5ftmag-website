ADOX Scala 160

A black-and-white slide film made by reversal-processing Adox's Silvermax base. Rated ISO 160 in daylight, it can also be shot as a negative at ISO 80 to 100. Clear triacetate base with an anti-halation layer. Supply is limited since Silvermax was discontinued.
