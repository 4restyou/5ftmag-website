AGFA

Agfa Scala 200X

The only black-and-white slide film ever sold commercially. It develops to a positive rather than a negative, so you can lay it on a lightbox and see the image as is. It required its own AP-44 process, which meant sending it to designated labs. Grain is very fine and blacks are deep, though it has a reputation for thin midtones. Rated at ISO 200, it could be shot anywhere from 100 to 1600. Discontinued in 2005.
