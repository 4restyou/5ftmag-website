Lucky Color 200

The first color line from China's Lucky since its color negative, discontinued in 2012, came back in the first half of 2025. ISO 200, standard C-41 processing. Expect neutral tones with a slight red cast, fine grain and punchy contrast. Released in both 35mm and 120.
