CHINESE CINESTILL

Chinese CineStill 800T

A fast, tungsten-balanced film that comes alive in low light full of artificial sources, like night streets and city skylines. This line comes from Chinese OEM factories that cut Kodak Vision3 family motion picture film, strip the remjet, and respool it into 36-exposure 35mm cassettes. In Korea it goes by a nickname that means "Chinese CineStill." Since it's a generic name rather than an official brand, color and quality can vary from batch to batch. There's no DX code, so you have to set the ISO manually, and any regular C-41 lab can develop it.
