ORWO Wolfen NC500

ORWO's color negative cinema film, revived in 2023. Its cinematic teal shadows and warm highlights make a strong impression. ECN-2 processing is the original recommendation, but it can also go through standard C-41, which makes it a good way to bring a cine look into everyday photos.
