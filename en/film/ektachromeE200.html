Kodak Ektachrome E200

An ISO 200 slide film from the Kodak Professional line. It held up well to pushing (+1 to +3 stops), so it was often used in low light, in the studio, and under artificial light, and its calm tones and fine grain won it fans. Discontinued in 2012.
