11 camera repair shops in Seoul

Places that take film cameras for repair. Models handled and costs can change, so it is worth asking before you go.

Contact: 02-779-4305 · 010-5246-4305

Tricky repairs such as lens haze, balsam separation, and focus calibration, large-aperture lens cleaning, and flash and electronic circuit work. Also machines parts in-house on a lathe and milling machine.

Namdaemun Speed

Unit 301, 3F, 2-1 Namdaemun-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul
