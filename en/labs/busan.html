3 camera repair shops in Busan

Places that take film cameras for repair. Models handled and costs can change, so it is worth asking before you go.

Busan Sin Camera

47 Nampo-gil, Jung-gu, Busan

Specialty: Nikon, Olympus, Contax

Contact: 051-246-0360

A long-established camera repair shop in Nampo-dong. Known for repairs on the Olympus PEN F and FT, Contax cables, and more. Offers a 2-year repair warranty, consultation by text message, courier drop-off, and card payment. Terms may change, so call before dropping off a camera.

Jungang Camera

Inside Mijin Arcade, 8 Junggu-ro 43beon-gil, Jung-gu, Busan

Specialty: Film and digital camera repair

Contact: 051-248-2624

Inside Mijin Arcade in Bupyeong Kkangtong Market, across from the entrance to Gukje Market sections 2 and 3. Repairs both film and digital cameras and also sells cameras. The space is small, and service can be slow while they are focused on repairs, so allow plenty of time. Ask ahead whether the repair is possible and whether parts are in stock.

Chalkak Isangja

2F, 23 Jaesong 1-ro, Haeundae-gu, Busan

Specialty: Manual film cameras, rangefinders, lenses

Contact: 010-4582-8958

In Jaesong-dong, Haeundae. Works mainly on manual film cameras. It publishes a list of the models it handles, including the Nikon F, FM, and FE, Canon A series and Canonet, Pentax SP, K, and M, Minolta X300 and SRT, Olympus PEN and Trip 35, and Yashica Electro, along with Ricoh, Mamiya, Konica, Fujica, Praktica, Contax, and Topcon. Services range from inspection and cleaning to full overhauls. Also does lens cleaning and aperture and focus calibration. Repair discounts for minor brands and manual models such as the Cosina CT-1, Cobica 35, Yashica FX-3, and Ricoh XR-5. The published model list and price list date from April 2021 and may have changed. Ask whether the repair is possible and what it costs before you go.
