About

5FT.MAG / ABOUT
A ROLL OF FILM

One roll of film is
exactly 5ft long, about 1.5m.

We named the magazine after that length: a familiar roll of 35mm film, just enough for 36 frames. We wanted to share what draws us to analog and to film cameras, and how the same film takes on completely different colors and stories depending on who shoots it and where.

Each issue of 5ft magazine is built around a single film. Three photographers, one writer and one illustrator each tell a story through their own eyes. Same film, different stories. That is what we want to say.

Through film, an analog medium, 5ft magazine will keep sharing its warmth and its small discoveries with our readers.

Editor-in-chiefPark Soon Yeol
OUR BELIEF

Same film, different eyes.

How it works

How an issue of 5ft magazine comes together

01

Choosing the film

The editors pick the film and the theme at the center of the issue.

02

Shooting

Three photographers shoot the same film, each with their own subject and point of view.

03

Developing & scanning

The rolls are developed and scanned, bringing out the grain and color only film has.

04

Words and drawings

A writer and an illustrator create text and drawings that grow out of the photographs.

05

Publishing

When an issue is truly ready, we publish it digitally and in print.
